## Deviations and decisions

| # | Item | Plan | Built | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Keys of `prep`, `cycle` and `drift` refs | `<ms>:<kind>:<business id>` | The row's uuid is the business id (`isms:prep:<uuid>`); `RecordRef.code` is the tuple's first element (title, requirement code, entity code) | The tuples have no unique business id (titles repeat, cycle items share `-`). WP-39's `projectSnapshot` keeps these refs by `ref.code`, so owner scoping works unchanged |
| 2 | Known parity differences (J.4) | Exact equality | The parity test aligns and asserts these separately: `rina` is also a member of `tst`, the last entry of `orgs` (G.3); for bsd and rmn the licence card names the organisation's own licence and counts its own systems, and `TITLES` and `CLAUSE_AT_RISK` cover its own systems (the sample copies the whole sample's card, titles and clauses into every organisation, WP-07 deviation 3); rmn lists its unused Managed Vault (A-41: every organisation has one; the sample lists used repositories only); `generatedAt` is removed (clock) | Each follows from a plan rule; ndt has no difference except `orgs` |
| 3 | `ChainItem.ref`, `Activity.doc` | J.4 removes them | The comparator first checks that the sample's `ref` equals the API's `refKey` and that the sample's `doc` key names the API's `docId`, then removes them | Stronger check of the wire form the web rehydrates (H.4) |
| 4 | `MS[].approver` | Not in G.5 | The approver of the system's first own document (by position) that names one | The sample carries it (UI 2.14) and J.4 does not remove it |
| 5 | Bilingual texts kept on the system | `EVENTS.t` from `short_label`, `result` from `outcome_summary`, `ROUTING` note from the rule, `MS.lead` from the next event | When `management_system.display.eventLabels` (by date), `display.routingNote` or `display.lead` hold a pair whose English text equals the stored text, the pair is returned; `EVENTS[].short` comes from the same labels | The stored columns are English only; WP-14 keeps the sample's pairs in `display` (D-06). An edited text no longer matches the pair, so the stored text wins |
| 6 | Stored task items (D-29) | `kind: 'task'`, key `<ms>:task:<uuid>` | `title` is the record code (else the task title), `act` the task title in both languages, `stage` null, `st` and `after` as for chain items | The controller shows `act` as the heading and `title` as the detail for unknown kinds |
| 7 | Drift row without due date | `[code, title, holder, due_at]` | The local date the row was detected stands in for a missing `due_at` | The wire tuple needs a date (`DriftRow`) |
| 8 | Documents of a system | Documents linked to the system | Linked documents plus organisation-wide documents without a link | `is_org_wide` documents belong to every system (C.9) |
| 9 | Evidence never produced | E.3 band reads an age | `critical` unless per event | No production date means nothing to rely on |
| 10 | `TITLES` before scoping | Titles of the visible chain records | Titles of every chain record of the organisation; `filterOrgSnapshot` keeps only the codes of the caller's visible chains | One rule for every reader; the scope policy decides visibility |
| 11 | J.4 "before and after running every job once" | My Tasks counts after the jobs | Asserted before the jobs only | The jobs are WP-22 and WP-23 (wave 8 and 9); see follow-ups |

## Follow-ups

- WP-20 (verify command, BR-01): use `entryEvidenceState` from `src/modules/read-model` (or `read-model/derive/freshness`) over the entry's non-deleted evidence with `thresholdsFor` and the pack period; `satisfiesBr01` decides the verification. `isUnverifiable`, `validForStatus` and `br01LinkOk` are in `derive/integrity.ts`.
- WP-22 (`freshness.recompute`, already importing `derive/freshness` and `derive/integrity`) and WP-23 (`drift.evaluate`): the drift predicates are in `derive/drift.ts` (`isStaleRisk` with `predicate.months`, `lacksValidDueDiligence`, `unverifiableEvidenceOf`, `evidenceWithoutHashOf`, `isStaleMetric`, `lacksScheduledExercise`). Add the J.4 "after every job once" assertion to `test/int/parity/reference-numbers.test.ts` (or a sibling file) once the jobs run in tests: the My Tasks counts and `DRIFT` rows must stay as they are (G.9).
- WP-25 (API data source): `ChainItem.refKey` and `Activity.docId` name records of the same bundle; `prep`, `cycle` and `drift` refs are found by kind and `ref.code` (deviation 1). `GET /tasks` items carry `ms` and `key`; stored tasks have kind `task`. `ROLES` outside demo mode lists the user's own UI roles.
- WP-32 (per-screen endpoints): `ReadModelService.systemSnapshotIn(trx, org, ms)` gives the unscoped snapshot in the caller's transaction; `portfolioCounts`, `openPerClause`, `maturityValues`, `clauseLevel`, `overviewBlockers`, `nextGate`, `currentPhaseNo` are the UI 2.6 and 2.8 derivations.
- WP-30: the scoping cases of J.5 for `GET` routes can reuse `test/int/read-model/helpers.ts` (`createReadModelApp`, `sessionFor` for demo users, including the `tst` external auditor with its grant).
- Orchestrator: J.4 could list deviation 2 (the sample's organisation-wide copies of licence, titles and clauses, and `rina`'s `tst` membership) so later parity work starts from it. The type check of `apps/server/test/int/jobs/tasks-overdue.test.ts` (WP-22, in progress) failed once on a missing `.js` extension during this work; it passed in the final run.
